16-Pin IDC Cable Tester

A standalone high-speed diagnostic tester for 16-pin ribbon cables using NE555 timer clocking, dual CD4017 decade counters, LM7805 voltage regulation, and 16 LED line diagnostics.

Hardware / Sequential Logic

Digital Dice

An interactive electronic die powered by an NE555 astable oscillator, 74LS74 D flip-flops, a 4510N counter, and a 74LS47N BCD-to-7-segment decoder to display randomized values (1–6) on an HD-H101 display upon tactile switch triggering.
