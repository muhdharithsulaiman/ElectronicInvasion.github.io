About Me

MUHAMMAD HARITH BIN SULAIMAN

Welcome to Electronic Invasion. I am a qualified Electronic Engineer holding a Diploma in Technology Electronic from KV Kuala Klawang and a Bachelor's Degree in Technology & Engineering Electronic System from UniMAP.

With extensive hands-on experience in electronic engineering and system design, I specialize in transforming ambitious concepts into fully functional, high-performance physical prototypes. My mission is to empower students and researchers by providing top-tier Final Year Project (FYP) consultancy and technical execution at student-friendly, affordable rates.

Beyond building the physical hardware, I offer comprehensive end-to-end guidance through every single stage of project development:

1. Ideation & Concept

Feasibility assessment and logic flow design tailored to project goals.

2. Schematic Design

Precise circuit design ensuring optimal voltage regulation and signal integrity.

3. PCB Layout

Custom PCB design ready for fabrication and clean component mounting.

4. Bill of Materials (BOM)

Curated component selection maximizing performance while minimizing cost.

5. Custom Firmware & Code

Clean, documented programming tailored specifically to your target hardware.

FYP Projects

Hardware / Sequential Logic

16-Pin IDC Cable Tester

A standalone high-speed diagnostic tester for 16-pin ribbon cables using NE555 timer clocking, dual CD4017 decade counters, LM7805 voltage regulation, and 16 LED line diagnostics.

Hardware / Sequential Logic

Digital Dice

An interactive electronic die powered by an NE555 astable oscillator, 74LS74 D flip-flops, a 4510N counter, and a 74LS47N BCD-to-7-segment decoder to display randomized values (1–6) on an HD-H101 display upon tactile switch triggering.
